FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who is eligible for the TYC internship programme?

Students, freshers and aspiring professionals passionate about technology, design or business operations. If you are willing to learn, collaborate and contribute, you are welcome to apply.

Do I receive a certificate after completion?

Yes. Every intern who completes the programme receives an Official Internship Completion Certificate from Traya Yukti Core.

Is the internship paid?

Performance is recognised — stipends and rewards are offered as per company policy, based on your contribution during the programme.

What will I actually work on?

Real-world projects with professional mentorship — hands-on experience, portfolio-building opportunities and exposure to genuine business workflows.

How do I apply?

Apply through the official TYC internship application portal using the form below. Shortlisted candidates are contacted for the next round.
